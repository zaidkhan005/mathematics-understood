# Master Curriculum Map

`Mathematics_Understood_Master_Curriculum_Map_v0.1.xlsx` is the current planning workbook. Treat it as a draft, not as a final sequence of book volumes.

The map should eventually make explicit:

- concepts and prerequisite dependencies;
- entry points, diagnostics, and repair paths;
- problem-first opportunities and likely productive struggle;
- equation/model construction outcomes;
- proof/reasoning expectations and progression;
- essential diagrams/graphs plus reader-generated visual practice;
- relevant scientific applications;
- assessment/transfer checks and verification needs;
- status, open questions, and decision history.

When revising the workbook, preserve its existing structure unless the revision is deliberate; record material changes in `decisions/decision-log.md`.

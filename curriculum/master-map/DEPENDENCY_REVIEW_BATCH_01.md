## Recommendations

### EDGE-029 — FND-015: Rational numbers and signed arithmetic → ALG-001: Variables, unknowns and parameters

**Draft classification:** Helpful

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Signed-rational arithmetic helps with later examples but is not needed to introduce the roles of variable, unknown, and parameter. Use whole-number contexts first; provide FND-015 as a repair path where calculations—not letter roles—block learning.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Teach the letter-role objective with whole-number contexts, then test a separate signed-rational transfer item. If only the second fails, do not treat FND-015 as a gate for ALG-001.

### EDGE-030 — FND-001: Mathematical communication and notation → ALG-001: Variables, unknowns and parameters

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Minimum symbol/statement literacy supports reading letters and translating between verbal and symbolic forms. For accessible self-study, teach this minimum within an ALG-001 bridge instead of denying entry.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Diagnostic: interpret x + 2 and translate 'a number plus 2'; provide a short notation bridge and retest variable/unknown/parameter distinctions.

### EDGE-031 — ALG-001: Variables, unknowns and parameters → ALG-002: Expressions and equivalence

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** The meanings of variables and parameters are needed to interpret symbolic expressions and reason about equivalence; the two concepts may be scaffolded in adjacent lessons.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Ask learners to identify the role of a letter and explain why 2x + 3 and 3 + 2x are equivalent; record whether an ALG-001 refresh is enough.

### EDGE-032 — FND-004: Whole-number operations and properties → ALG-002: Expressions and equivalence

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Numerical operation meanings and basic properties underpin interpretation and simplification of symbolic expressions. This requires operational meaning, not mastery of every arithmetic strand.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Move from 3 + 2 and 3 × 2 to 3x + 2x and equivalent forms; distinguish operation-meaning errors from notation errors.

### EDGE-033 — FND-014: Powers, roots and integer exponents → ALG-002: Expressions and equivalence

**Draft classification:** Helpful

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Exponent notation appears in some expressions, but initial expression meaning/equivalence can be learned without exponent laws. Introduce FND-014 just in time when powers enter.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Check the ALG-002 outcomes using expressions without exponents; verify that power laws are not secretly assumed before introducing them explicitly.

### EDGE-034 — ALG-002: Expressions and equivalence → ALG-003: Equality, equations and inequalities as statements

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Equations and inequalities relate expressions and constrain possible values, so expression interpretation and equivalence are foundational to the current mapped outcome.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Ask learners to interpret x + 2 = 5 and x + 2 < 5, distinguish expression from statement, and test candidate values.

### EDGE-035 — FND-006: Number line, integers, opposites and distance → ALG-003: Equality, equations and inequalities as statements

**Draft classification:** Helpful

**Specific dependency mechanism:** A signed number line is important for representing solution sets with negative values, but the meaning of an equation or inequality as a statement can be taught earlier. This cluster combines both tasks.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Assess separately: identify an equation/inequality as a constraint, then graph a solution set spanning negative and positive values. FND-006 is more essential for the latter.

### EDGE-036 — ALG-003: Equality, equations and inequalities as statements → ALG-004: Solving linear equations and inequalities

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Solving equations depends on understanding them as constraints and preserving the solution set under valid transformations.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Have learners solve and verify a linear equation, then explain why a valid operation performed on both sides preserves equality.

### EDGE-037 — FND-015: Rational numbers and signed arithmetic → ALG-004: Solving linear equations and inequalities

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Full linear equation/inequality fluency with standard rational coefficients uses signed and rational arithmetic. Integer-only starter examples may precede it, but the complete ALG-004 outcome needs this capability or targeted repair.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Use equations with negative/fractional coefficients and sign-sensitive inequalities. Diagnose arithmetic errors separately and provide an FND-015 repair where needed.

### EDGE-038 — ALG-004: Solving linear equations and inequalities → ALG-005: Rearranging formulas and literal equations

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Rearranging literal formulas extends isolating an unknown through reversible equation transformations, the central capability developed in linear equation solving.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Rearrange a familiar formula for a different variable, substitute back, and check nonzero-denominator restrictions.

### EDGE-039 — ALG-002: Expressions and equivalence → ALG-005: Rearranging formulas and literal equations

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Formula rearrangement requires learners to interpret and maintain equivalent symbolic expressions even when the target variable changes.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Give an equivalent-expression task before rearranging a literal formula; check whether the transformation reasoning transfers.

### EDGE-040 — FND-013: Measurement, units, conversion and dimensional sense → ALG-005: Rearranging formulas and literal equations

**Draft classification:** Helpful

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Unit conversion and dimensional sense are not prerequisites for symbolic rearrangement itself; they are important for interpreting unit-bearing formulas and scientific applications.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Compare a pure-symbol rearrangement with a unit-bearing application; assess algebraic success separately from interpreting and checking units.

### EDGE-041 — FND-006: Number line, integers, opposites and distance → ALG-006: Coordinate plane and plotting points

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** The coordinate-plane outcome includes signed coordinates and points in all quadrants. Understanding signed values relative to zero is a direct conceptual support and can be bridged locally.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Ask learners to order signed values on a number line, then plot ordered pairs in all four quadrants; identify sign and axis errors.

### EDGE-042 — FND-001: Mathematical communication and notation → ALG-006: Coordinate plane and plotting points

**Draft classification:** Helpful

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Basic notation helps with ordered pairs and coordinate labels, but these conventions are teachable inside the coordinate lesson. Do not make the entire FND-001 cluster a rigid gate.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Give a brief ordered-pair/axis primer and test plotting; compare error types before and after the local explanation.

### EDGE-043 — ALG-006: Coordinate plane and plotting points → ALG-007: Graph reading and construction

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Graph literacy explicitly requires plotting/interpreting coordinates and relating graphs to tables, equations, and contexts; coordinate-plane fluency is a direct dependency.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Before graph-construction tasks, plot table values with labelled axes and scales; assess coordinate plotting separately from trend interpretation.

### EDGE-044 — FND-012: Proportional relationships and percentages → ALG-007: Graph reading and construction

**Draft classification:** Helpful

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Proportional relationships offer useful graphs through the origin, but graph literacy must cover non-proportional, nonlinear, and empirical graphs too.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Test graph interpretation on non-proportional lines, nonlinear curves, and empirical/categorical data as well as percentage examples.

### EDGE-045 — ALG-007: Graph reading and construction → ALG-008: Rates, slope and proportional versus affine relations

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** The current slope/rate outcome includes graph interpretation and proportional-versus-affine comparison; graph literacy supports axis/scale reading and representation changes.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Infer rate from a graph with nontrivial axis scales, confirm from a table, and separate graph-reading errors from rate-reasoning errors.

### EDGE-046 — FND-011: Ratios, rates and unit rates → ALG-008: Rates, slope and proportional versus affine relations

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Slope/rate of change is change-per-change; ratios and unit rates supply the conceptual basis for interpreting slope rather than memorizing rise-over-run.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Transfer from a unit-rate context and double number line to a table and graph; explain the ratio and its units.

### EDGE-047 — FND-013: Measurement, units, conversion and dimensional sense → ALG-008: Rates, slope and proportional versus affine relations

**Draft classification:** Helpful

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Unit/dimensional sense improves interpretation of slope and intercept units, but an initial slope concept can be taught on a unitless graph. The full measurement cluster is broader than the minimum prerequisite.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Assess calculation first and unit interpretation second; teach only the missing unit subskill locally when needed.

### EDGE-048 — ALG-002: Expressions and equivalence → ALG-009: Functions as input-output rules

**Draft classification:** Helpful

**Specific dependency mechanism:** A function can first be introduced through mappings, input-output tables, and uniqueness, without symbolic expressions. Expression skill is needed for formula representations.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Teach a mapping/table example before a formula; assess function recognition separately from interpretation of symbolic rules.

### EDGE-049 — ALG-006: Coordinate plane and plotting points → ALG-009: Functions as input-output rules

**Draft classification:** Helpful

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Coordinates are needed for graphical representations of functions, not for the input-output/mapping definition itself. Multiple representations should provide more than one entry route.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Assess function recognition from mappings and tables before graph tasks; keep graph interpretation as a dependent sub-outcome.

### EDGE-050 — ALG-008: Rates, slope and proportional versus affine relations → ALG-010: Linear functions and intercepts

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** The mapped linear-function outcome uses slope/rate and the proportional-versus-affine distinction established in ALG-008.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** From a table with constant first differences, build a linear rule, identify slope/intercept, and distinguish a line through the origin from one with nonzero intercept.

### EDGE-051 — ALG-009: Functions as input-output rules → ALG-010: Linear functions and intercepts

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** In this curriculum, a general function concept supplies input-output, domain, and range language needed to position linear functions as one function family. Motivation with a linear example can still come first.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Compare linear and non-linear functions and a relation that fails uniqueness; check whether learners can explain the special case and its domain.

### EDGE-052 — ALG-003: Equality, equations and inequalities as statements → ALG-010: Linear functions and intercepts

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Interpreting a linear function as an equation requires minimum fluency with equality as a relation and symbolic statements. Teach that minimum within the linear-function lesson if necessary.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Ask learners to read y = 2x + 3 as a relationship, explain what equality asserts, and interpret slope/intercept; note if a short bridge suffices.

### EDGE-053 — ALG-004: Solving linear equations and inequalities → ALG-011: Systems of equations and inequalities

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Substitution and elimination extend equation-solving transformations and depend on preserving equality while isolating or eliminating variables.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Have learners solve/check a one-variable linear equation before a system; code errors as equation-solving, sign, or system-model issues.

### EDGE-054 — ALG-006: Coordinate plane and plotting points → ALG-011: Systems of equations and inequalities

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** The mapped systems outcome explicitly includes graphical solutions, which require coordinate plotting and reading intersection points. Algebraic methods may be introduced separately.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Solve a two-line system graphically and algebraically, then compare the intersection with the ordered-pair solution.

### EDGE-055 — ALG-010: Linear functions and intercepts → ALG-011: Systems of equations and inequalities

**Draft classification:** Core required

**Specific dependency mechanism:** Linear-function representations supply the line equations and intersection meaning used in graphical systems. This is core to the full multi-representation outcome, though substitution/elimination can be taught separately.

**Proposed validation check (not yet run):** Plot two linear functions, identify their intersection, and substitute the ordered pair into both equations; assess algebra and graph-reading separately.

## What this batch suggests

1. Several relationships are prerequisites for the **full stated outcome**, not for an introductory example. Keep early entry routes available.
2. Notation and variables can be co-taught; prerequisite graphs should not force learners to master every notation convention before encountering algebra.
3. Signed/rational arithmetic matters for the full scope of linear-equation solving, but starter equations can be used earlier.
4. Coordinate plotting across all quadrants requires signed-coordinate understanding; graph literacy and functions have multiple non-graphical entry routes.
5. Proportional reasoning is important for slope but is not a universal prerequisite for reading all graph types.

## Acceptance rule

Record their review separately from this AI-assisted draft, and only mark an edge confirmed when the rationale is defensible and the relevant diagnostics/pilots have been considered.

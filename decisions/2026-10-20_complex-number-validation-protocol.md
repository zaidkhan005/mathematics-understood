# Decision note — Complex-number validation protocol v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** Proposed test plan; not piloted  
**Related decision:** D-11, the provisional separation of CA-001 algebraic complex numbers, CA-003 complex-plane/polar representation, and CA-002 complex analysis.

## Decision

Create and use a formative validation protocol before changing the v0.3 curriculum graph again. The existing v0.3 workbook and dependency classifications remain unchanged.

## Rationale

The next useful evidence is not another structural graph check. It is whether learners can reach the scoped CA-001 algebraic outcomes without prior polar-form knowledge, and how the individual CA-003 sub-skills depend on coordinate, distance/Pythagorean and trigonometric knowledge.

The protocol deliberately separates:
- prior knowledge needed for the full outcome from a hard entry gate;
- plotting from modulus and argument/polar conversion;
- independent transfer from success on guided examples; and
- observations from interpretations of learner errors.

## Scope and record

See [Validation Protocol v0.1](../curriculum/master-map/VALIDATION_PROTOCOL_COMPLEX_NUMBER_SPLIT_v0.1.md) for diagnostic items, planned lesson sequence, independent transfer tasks, error codes, edge-by-edge questions, data capture and provisional decision logic.

## Limits and next steps

- The protocol is not yet piloted; no learner outcomes are claimed.
- A mathematics educator should check the tasks, answer keys and thresholds before testing.
- Early try-outs are formative, not representative or causal evidence.
- Educator interpretation and learner evidence must be recorded before revising the map.
- CA-002 dependency claims (EDGE-259–261 and EDGE-273) require separate testing; the CA-001/CA-003 protocol cannot validate them.
- Any later workbook revision must preserve v0.2/v0.3 history, be versioned, and undergo a fresh structural audit.

## Status

Open pending educator review and learner evidence.

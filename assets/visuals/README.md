# Visual asset system

Store editable/source files separately from publication exports. Use stable names such as `V01-C04-FIG03-fraction-number-line.svg`.

For each important visual, record: asset ID, chapter use, learning purpose, source/editable format, export format, alt text or text equivalent, factual/mathematical check, rights status, and review status. Graphs must be checked against their equations/data; diagrams must not imply more than the definitions or assumptions warrant.

Visuals teach learners to see, but must not become a permanent crutch. Embed prompts that ask the reader to predict, sketch, complete, reconstruct, interpret, or alter a representation before showing the completed version where pedagogically appropriate. Provide descriptions or other access routes for learners who do not experience vivid mental imagery.

# Rights, attribution, and reuse

No public reuse license has been chosen for this project. Until the owners make and document a licensing decision:

- Keep the repository private unless there is a deliberate decision to publish it.
- Do not assume that project content is available for unrestricted reuse or redistribution.
- Track the original source, license, attribution, and permission status for every third-party image, diagram, exercise, excerpt, dataset, or other reused material.
- Prefer original explanations and visuals.
- Keep proof of permissions and license terms in the source register when relevant.

This document records a project process rule; it is not legal advice.

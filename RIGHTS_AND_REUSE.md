# Rights, attribution, and reuse

This repository is public. This document explains the project's intended licensing scope; it is not legal advice.

- [Official license summary](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- [Full legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode)
- Reusers may share and adapt covered material, including commercially, subject to the license.
- Reusers must provide appropriate attribution, link to the license, and indicate whether changes were made. They must not imply endorsement by the project authors.

## What is not automatically covered

The repository-wide notice does **not** automatically license:

- third-party images, diagrams, excerpts, datasets, exercises, fonts, papers, or other assets, each of which remains subject to its own license, permissions, and attribution requirements;
- software, source code, or executable code, unless the relevant file expressly states its license;
- project names, logos, or other marks as trademarks;
- patents, privacy/publicity rights, or other rights not granted by CC BY 4.0;
- any material that a contributor does not have authority to license.

A file-specific license or rights notice takes precedence for that file. If a work includes third-party material, clearly mark the material and record its source, license, attribution, and permission status in the source register where relevant. Do not include material with unclear reuse rights until the issue is resolved or the material is replaced.

## Contributions

Contributors should submit only material they have the right to share. Unless a different arrangement is agreed in writing before acceptance, submitting an original contribution intended for the project means it may be distributed under CC BY 4.0 within the scope described above. Contributors must identify third-party material and its conditions before it is accepted.

## Important boundary

Making a repository public does not by itself grant a license. Reuse permissions are limited to rights that the relevant authors or contributors can legally grant. CC BY 4.0 is generally irrevocable for compliant uses of material already offered under the license. This document is a practical project policy, not legal advice.
